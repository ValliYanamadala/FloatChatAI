# Geographic And Ocean Terms

Project examples include region names such as Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, Indian Ocean, and equator.

A region is a named area used for filtering, comparison, or map display.

A location may be a named place, such as Chennai, or explicit latitude and longitude coordinates.

The query-understanding layer must not fabricate geographic coordinates or radii. If a user says "near me" without user coordinates, clarification is required.

Float IDs and platform numbers must be distinguished from place names.
